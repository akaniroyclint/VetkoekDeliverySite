Our Story

Vetkoek Delivery started with a simple goal — bringing township flavor straight to your door. We serve fresh vetkoek, burgers, and kotas made with love and delivered hot.

Our Mission

We believe in making authentic South African street food accessible to everyone. Our mission is to deliver meals that are not only delicious but also affordable, fast, and made with care.

Our Values

  • Freshness: Every order is prepared with fresh ingredients daily.
  • Community: We support local suppliers and proudly serve Nelspruit neighborhoods.
  • Quality: We never compromise on taste or service.
  • Convenience: Easy ordering, fast delivery, and friendly service.

Meet the Team

Our team is made up of passionate cooks, delivery drivers, and customer service staff who share one vision: bringing joy to your table. We’re proud to be a local business serving our community.

Community Connection

Vetkoek Delivery isn’t just about food — it’s about people. We regularly participate in local events, sponsor community initiatives, and aim to give back by supporting youth programs and small businesses.
